# ADR-0009: No Unverified Benchmark Claims

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-16

## Decision

README, resume, and marketing material may include only reproducible, reviewed measurements with environment and methodology.

## Rationale

Unqualified numbers are misleading and undermine the engineering purpose of the project.

## Consequences

Phase 0 documents methodology only. Benchmark artifacts and claims are deferred until implementation and repeatable runs exist.
